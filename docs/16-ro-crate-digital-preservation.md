*Packaging your collection so it can be archived, reused and preserved beyond Omeka-S.*

## Why preservation matters for an Omeka-S collection

Using standard vocabularies in Omeka-S means a collection can be exported and archived in a form that works outside Omeka-S and lasts longer (see [Adding items](03-adding-items.md#property-labels-and-vocabularies)).
