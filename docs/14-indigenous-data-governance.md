# Indigenous data governance

*Respect the rights of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples in your collection.*

Many research collections include **Indigenous data**: cultural materials, knowledge, languages, images, recordings, or information about Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, their communities and their Country. Indigenous data governance means recognising that Indigenous peoples have the right to decide how this data is collected, described, accessed and used.

## Before you start

Guidance in this section is a starting point, not a substitute for working with the communities connected to your collection. Before you describe or publish Indigenous data:

- **Work with communities** to understand what can be shared, how it should be described and who has authority to decide.
- **Follow your institution's protocols.** Many universities and organisations have their own ICIP or Indigenous data sovereignty protocols. Check with your Indigenous strategy or research office.
- **Follow the AIATSIS Code of Ethics.** The [AIATSIS Code of Ethics for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Research](https://aiatsis.gov.au/research/ethical-research/code-ethics) sets out expectations for research involving Indigenous peoples.
- **Use the [Pre-publication checklist](08-pre-publication-checklist.md)** before making any collection containing Indigenous data public.
