# Using AI and acknowledging AI use

*Using generative AI responsibly when building a collection, and saying clearly when you have.*

> 🚧 **This page is an outline.** Sections marked ✏️ **Content needed** are still to be written.

## Ways AI might be used in an Omeka-S project

> ✏️ **Content needed:** Examples of common uses, and when each is or isn't appropriate:
> - Drafting or enriching descriptions and metadata
> - Writing alt text for images
> - Data cleaning and standardisation (see [Data cleaning](09-data-cleaning.md))
> - Transcription and translation
> - Generating diagrams, such as data models
> - Writing code or scripts for import and export

## Risks and limitations

> ✏️ **Content needed:**
> - Accuracy: errors and invented facts, references or identifiers
> - Bias and the misrepresentation of people and communities
> - Indigenous data and cultural material: why AI tools should not be used on this material without community authority (see [CARE principles](13-care-principles.md))
> - Privacy and confidentiality: what must not be uploaded to AI tools
> - Copyright and licensing of AI inputs and outputs

## Acknowledging AI use

### Where to acknowledge it

> ✏️ **Content needed:** Guidance on where to place acknowledgements:
> - The site's About or Methodology page (collection-level statement)
> - Documentation such as data models and metadata schemas
> - Individual items, for example in a provenance or notes field, where AI was used for that record
> - Deposits and DOI metadata (see [Persistent identifiers](15-persistent-identifiers.md))

> 🖼️ **Image needed:** An example About page showing an AI acknowledgement statement.

### What to include

> ✏️ **Content needed:** The elements of a good acknowledgement: which tool and version, what it was used for, which parts of the work it affected, and how its output was checked.

### Example statements

An example from a Curated Collections metadata schema:

> AI was NOT used to prepare this metadata scheme, with the exception of the diagram of the data model, which was generated by Claude.
