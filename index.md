# Curated Collections Omeka Documentation

A help guide to building, describing and publishing research collections with [Omeka-S](https://omeka.org/s/). It is written for users of the Curated Collections platform, but most of the guidance applies to any Omeka-S installation. Where something is specific to Curated Collections, the guide says so.

> 🚧 **This guide is in development.** An introduction and further sections are coming soon.
